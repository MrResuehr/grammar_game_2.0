Grammar notebook

Simple Present — habits, facts and routines.

I/you/we/they work. He/she/it works. After he/she/it: go → goes, study → studies.

Negatives: don't / doesn't + base verb.
Questions: Do / Does + subject + base verb?

Examples: The clock ticks every day. It doesn't stop. Does it work?

Present Progressive

Actions happening now: am/is/are + verb-ing. I am working. She is baking. They are playing.

Negatives: She isn’t baking. Questions: Is she baking?

Every day: She bakes bread. Right now: She is baking bread.
